This article covers a speculative system that would allow us to circumvent the heat death of the universe and live for an arbitrarily long time in realities of our own design. It was was largely motivated by a recent playthrough of [SOMA](https://store.steampowered.com/app/282140/SOMA/).

<!-- more -->

## Introduction

The solution proposed (for escaping the heat death of the universe) in this article has the following dependencies:

1. That consciousness is an emergent phenomena of medium independent computation.
2. Femtomachines.
3. Negative mass.
    3.1. That negative mass mass can be concentrated into a singularity.
    3.2. That the concentration of negative mass into a singularity would result in inverted time dilation, when contrasted with a classical singularity.

As such, it falls firmly in the domain of science fiction.

Additionally, one other constraint on this solution is that while it would allow us to extend consciousness an arbitrary amount of time into the future, it would not facilitate the extension of consciousness an *infinite* amount of time into the future.

## Femtomachines

Femtomachines (to the best of my knowledge) originate in Greg Egans "Schilds Ladder". They are similar to the sophons from Cixin Liu's "Three Body Problem" (and the technology is explored in various other science fiction stories to boot).

The basic idea is that you have compressed high performance computing technology into some incredibly small form factor - on the order of at most protons, or ideally smaller.
